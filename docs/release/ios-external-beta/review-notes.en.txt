AskHuman is a companion for developer-owned Macs running AskHuman and AI coding agents. It is not a standalone chat service. There is no app login, shared account or in-app demo mode. Full functionality requires a Mac and a separately configured MQTT broker. The iOS app is supplied through TestFlight; the reviewer does not need to compile or sign the iOS app.

Setup guide: https://naituw.github.io/AskHuman/beta/
Privacy policy: https://naituw.github.io/AskHuman/privacy/

Before submission, the developer must append the compatible Mac package download/source revision to these notes. Do not submit this draft with that information missing. Do not supply an expiring pairing QR code: the reviewer generates a new one on their own Mac.

Review steps:
1. Install the specified AskHuman Mac companion. Open AskHuman Settings → Channels → iOS App and click Start setup.
2. Configure your own MQTT broker (the card covers EMQX Serverless, flespi and Mosquitto, with the authentication and topic rules each needs), then run Test connection. Push notifications go through the push relay built into the Mac companion; the relay holds the developer's APNs signing credentials, and they are not needed on the reviewer's Mac.
3. In step 3 of the card, click Pair a new phone. In the iOS app, open Macs, Add a Mac and scan the QR code. Compare the six-digit codes and approve on the Mac within five minutes.
4. When setup is complete, click Send test question on the card, answer it on the phone and check that the card reports the answer. This test does not require any paid AI model request.
5. Close the app on the phone and send another test question. Check the notification, open it and answer. A test question is pushed at once; other questions wait fifteen minutes of Mac inactivity by default.
6. Session inspection and task creation require the reviewer's own configured agents. Remote task creation must also be enabled on the Mac. The Mac must remain online.

If this companion-device setup prevents review, please contact the review contact with the required access or demonstration material. We have not supplied a hosted shared Mac, broker account or demo environment in this submission.
