# Using the iOS app

[简体中文](./ios-usage.md) | English

For setup, see [iOS app channel setup](./ios-setup.en.md). This page covers what the phone can do once it is set up. Some features need both the Mac and the phone to run a recent AskHuman; where one side is too old the entry is missing and everything else works as before.

## Answering questions

- **Inbox**: questions from all your Macs, grouped by project and filterable by Mac. Open one to pick options, add a note, and attach images and files. A multi-question request is one vertical page whose bottom button reads "Next" until you have seen the last question and only then turns into "Send" (as in the Mac popup, so nothing goes out half-read); recommended options carry a star and are never preselected. After an answer goes out the app moves to the next request and tells you how many are left.
- **Confirmation pages**: actions that need your decision (permissions, Stop) have a dedicated page.
- **Switching between requests**: the arrows at the top right of a request open the previous or next one. Drafts, attachments and reading positions are kept, and uploads and replies can finish in the background.
- **Answering across channels**: when the same question shows up in the Mac popup, Feishu and the phone, the first answer wins and the others are told it was handled.
- **Quick replies**: the button to the left of "Send" opens a menu. The lower part is the presets (edited in Settings → General → Quick replies on the Mac; read-only on the phone): one tap sends it, replying with that sentence to every question you have not answered yet. The upper part has the two directives "Away mode" and "Stop using AskHuman": picking one parks it above the bar and it only takes effect when you send this answer. What's next and select-only questions have no presets.
- **Annotations**: select a passage in the Agent's message and tap "Add annotation" in the system menu to write your note. When you send the answer, the notes go to the Agent together with the passage each one is about, so a note alone is enough to send. The "Annotations" button in the answer bar (with a count) lists them; edit, delete or jump to the passage, at most 20 per answer, and annotations you have not sent stay in the phone's draft. It needs a current Mac and a message rendered as Markdown; confirmation pages and select-only questions do not have it.
- **Session history**: when the request comes from a real Agent session, pull down at the top of the request page (or use "⋯" → "Session history") to list that session's earlier exchanges above the request (8 at a time; pull again at the top for older ones). They are read from the Mac's reply history, the same one the Mac's history window shows, which keeps only the number of entries set in the settings (200 by default); the phone does not keep it. When the Mac is offline the row says "Can't reach the Mac" with "Try again". A request that names no session (an MCP call that cannot tell its session id) has no such entry.
- **On the phone**: answered requests and attachments are kept locally.

## Notifications

- Every question shows up in the phone's inbox as soon as it is asked, with nothing to "activate" or "take over"; open the app any time to see and answer it.
- The system notification only follows once you have been away from the Mac for a while: when the Mac's keyboard and mouse have been idle for the configured time (15 minutes by default) and you have not looked at the question on the phone, you get one notification and no reminders; while you are still working on the Mac nothing is pushed. To be told sooner, change **Push timing** on the iOS App card in Settings → Channels to 5 minutes or even "Immediately" (push as soon as a question is asked); the "The Mac has been idle for …" line there shows whether the detection works.

## When the Mac is offline

While a Mac cannot be reached, "Send" on a request page is dimmed (nothing is queued; your draft is kept) and there is nothing to cancel with, because the Agent cannot be told. Tap "**Remove for now ›**" after the line "The Mac is offline" at the bottom of the page, or "Remove for now" in the "⋯" menu, or swipe the row left in the inbox: the request is only taken off this phone's lists. **Neither the Mac nor the Agent is told, and the Agent keeps waiting.** When the Mac is back and the request is still waiting for you, it returns to the list by itself with an unread dot; one that has ended does not come back. States that recover by themselves (connecting, retrying, updating) do not offer this. While the Mac is online, cancelling is "⋯" → "Cancel request".

## Agents

- **Status**: the Agents tab shows every Mac session and its last result. A failed or incomplete result is marked in red or orange with its reason; to act on it, use the failure card in the inbox. Interrupted turns are marked "Interrupted" and send no result notification.
- **Live watching**: in a session's details; it only works while the app is in the foreground (up to two streams) and stops in the background.
- **Completion notifications**: tasks created from the phone, and new rounds started by a phone message or by "Request continuation" on a failure card, register one completion, failure or incomplete-result notification (up to 5 per Mac at a time). The rules are those of Notifications above: it waits for Mac idle time and is dropped when you return to the Mac first. A small read-only bell marks the sessions that will notify you; other Mac turns are not subscribed automatically.
- **Continue an existing session**: send an idle Codex Desktop, Cursor Desktop or live Pi session (with an updated extension) a message from its detail page; your unsent draft is preserved. Other sessions explain why they are unavailable. A Cursor Desktop chat can be sent to even when the Mac has marked it "ended" (after an hour of silence), but open it in Cursor first. To continue after a failure, choose "Request continuation" on the failure card in the inbox. Messages to a working session are queued and can be edited or withdrawn before the Agent reads them; once a native send is accepted it cannot be withdrawn, and if delivery is unknown, check the original chat before sending again. Both the app and the Mac need this update.
- **New task**: the "New task" button on the Agents tab: pick the Mac, project, Agent kind, the permission (ask before acting / run automatically; you must choose one) and optionally a todo, confirm with Face ID and the Mac launches it. Codex can use the desktop App or CLI, Cursor the CLI or the Agents window (experimental), other Agents use a terminal; having only the desktop app and no command-line tool is enough. You can attach camera photos, library photos or videos and files (up to 5 from the phone, each up to 10 MB; you still need to enter instructions or select a todo, and a todo's own attachments are sent along); an upload failure keeps the draft and does not start the task. With a supported Codex model you can turn on **Fast mode** (quicker, uses more of your allowance; it starts off for each new task).
  It needs "Allow creating Agent tasks from phone or IM" turned on on that Mac (which keeps its background service running and starting at login): the switch is on the iOS App card and on every IM channel card in Settings → Channels, in Settings → Advanced, and in the `AskHuman setup` wizard. While it is off the phone says "Agent tasks are turned off in AskHuman Settings on this Mac."
- **Away mode**: the moon button at the top right of the Agents tab (tap it again to end) opens a sheet where you pick the Macs it applies to and add a note. While it is on, every tab has an "Away mode is on" strip above the tab bar, and its "End" button turns it off in one tap. The Mac then answers Agent questions and What's next itself ("I'm away, carry on with your best judgement") instead of bothering you; an Agent whose original objective is complete or whose remaining work cannot proceed independently can reach you with `--escalate "reason"` (MCP: `escalate: "reason"`), with its reason shown before the report. A fourth consecutive request without a valid reason gets a clearly labeled system fallback, and the question page then opens with a note that can end away mode right there. The "AskHuman in this session" row on an Agent's detail page sets just that session to "Away" or "Not using AskHuman".

## Todos

- **Todos tab**: manage the todos on your Macs: browse projects, add, edit (tap), finish (swipe right), delete (swipe left, then tap Delete), reorder ("Edit" at the top right), switch ⚡ auto-run, and look through the history and restore from it. The menu in the title picks whose todos you see: one Mac (only the projects that have a folder on it), or **iCloud** (when you use iCloud todo sync: every synced project of your Apple ID, including ones that have no folder on the Mac in front of you; a Mac that has sync on and is online answers for it, and another one takes over when it drops).
- **Todos live on the Macs.** The phone keeps no copy, so the Mac you are looking at has to be online (when it is not, the page says so and the input bar is disabled; nothing is queued). The phone cannot create projects (a project is a folder on a Mac: open it under "Recent projects" or "Without todos" to give it its first todo). A change on the Mac shows up on the phone within a few seconds; a change from the phone is written to that Mac and, when iCloud todo sync is on, travels from there to your other Macs. A Mac whose AskHuman is too old to know about todos shows "Needs an update" in the menu.
- **Tidying todos on a request page**: on the "Todos" card of a question and the todo rows of What's next and of a Stop confirmation you can finish (swipe right), delete (swipe left, then tap Delete) and add ("+ Add todo" at the end of the list) right there; press and hold a row for "Complete / Copy / Delete". After completing, Undo stays available for a few seconds. Changes are written to the Mac the request came from; when it is offline or too old, the actions on the rows are dimmed with the reason and nothing is queued. Confirmation pages and select-only questions have no such list.
- **Picking todos while answering**: below the last question there is a collapsed "Todos" card (only when the request has a project that has todos, and the question is not select-only). Open it and tick todos like options; on send, the ticked ones are folded into the last answer and leave the queue, and a todo alone is enough to send. What's next shows the full queue: the first 5 by default, "Show all N todos" expands the rest. The Mac must be on a recent version too; an older Mac has no such card.

## Traffic and cost

- **Usage**: a phone in the background causes no sync traffic. Under heavy use (seven or eight Agents in parallel, the app opened about 70 times a day) the sync measured about 0.4 GB a month, with the Mac and the phone both on a recent version (counted on this device, where a message counts twice, published and delivered). **Attachments are the big part**: the phone downloads a picture from the Mac only when you open it in the viewer, and one screenshot of about 2 MB opened once counts about 7 MB by that count, so a month's total depends mostly on how many large pictures you open.
- **Reminders**: the Mac settings and the phone's Macs tab both show this month's traffic. When it gets close to each GB (from 0.8 GB by default) the inbox says so once: close the note at the top with its ✕ and it comes back only when the use gets close to the next GB. "Data use reminders" on the phone's Macs tab changes it to every 2 / 5 / 10 GB, or turns it off. The note only counts; it reads nothing from your broker and does not judge whether an allowance is used up, so a top-up or a plan change needs no action. It starts over each month (UTC).
- **Allowance**: whether your broker has a free allowance, how large it is and what happens when it runs out depends on the service you chose; see the [broker guide](./ios-broker.en.md). For the real usage on the broker's side, look in its console; the local count only covers this device's messages. When the broker refuses connections because an allowance is gone, the app says so: "the broker refused the connection, this month's free allowance may be used up". To use less, watch fewer live streams and open fewer large attachments.
- Using the public relay costs you nothing.

## Limits

- Live watching only works while the app is in the foreground and stops in the background.
- Codex Desktop chats accept additions while running and new messages while idle, including images and files, with the chat's existing settings; Desktop must stay connected on the Mac. Success means the app accepted the message, not that the model read it, and it cannot be withdrawn. If delivery is uncertain, inspect the original chat before choosing **I checked the original chat** (this prompt needs an updated phone app). Codex has no "failed" state, so there is no "failed" push for it.
- Withdrawing a queued message gives back the text only, not the attachments you had picked.
